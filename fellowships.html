The citizenship filter is the one to start with. The largest single source of postdoctoral salary support in the United States — the NIH F32 — requires citizenship or permanent residency, as does the NSF PRFB. A postdoc on a visa has a real but much shorter list, and it is worth knowing which programs are on it before writing anything.

Discontinued, dormant, or unreachable from here

These still appear on funding lists, university pages and older advice. They should not be planned around. Several were significant sources of support for exactly the people who can least afford to lose a cycle.

Ford Foundation Postdoctoral Fellowship (via the National Academies) — discontinued. The Foundation announced it is winding the program down by 2028; the last competition for new fellows was 2024, and it no longer appears on the National Academies' listing.

NSF PRFB Broadening Participation track — eliminated. Solicitation NSF 26-504 replaced all three previous competitive areas with a single focus on the intersection of artificial intelligence and the biological sciences. Award terms improved ($110,000/yr for 24 months, up from $80,000/yr for 36), but the addressable pool is far narrower. Anyone working from NSF 23-620 will submit a non-responsive proposal.

NIH K99/R00 citizenship — unresolved and worth watching. NIAID's guidance still states there is no citizenship requirement, while the NIH Grants Policy Statement describes a much narrower exception limited to applicants already in permanent-residency proceedings, and STAT reported in August 2026 that NIH intends to restrict eligibility to citizens and permanent residents. Nothing has been finalised in the NIH Guide. A visa-holding postdoc should confirm with the target Institute's program officer before building a submission around it.

Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships (Canada) — closed, and replaced by the Canada Postdoctoral Research Award (CAD 70,000/yr for two years), which is listed above.

Dormant or unverifiable: NYSCF Druckenmiller Fellowships (the page still says the RFA will not open); UNCF/Merck Postdoctoral Science Research Fellowship (only a decade-old page remains live); amfAR Mathilde Krim Fellowships (no open cycle since March 2025); the ASM/CDC Postdoctoral Research Fellowship (no live page — ASM currently offers no postdoctoral research fellowship); Simons Collaboration on the Global Brain postdoctoral fellowships (page removed); Simons Marine Microbial Ecology (last cycle 2024); Mark Foundation Fellow-to-Faculty; the AACR–Cancer Research UK Transatlantic Fellowships (the page still advertises the 2019 cycle); and Burroughs Wellcome's Postdoctoral Enrichment Program, whose page currently reads "Not Open to Applications" and is absent from their deadline listing.

Not available from a US institution: Wellcome Early-Career Awards and Cancer Research UK Career Development Fellowships both require a host organisation in the UK, Ireland or an eligible low- and middle-income country. Wellcome's eligible list narrows further from 29 October 2026. They are relocation routes, not fellowships a postdoc can hold here.

Method and maintenance

Every entry was checked against the sponsor's own page on 9 September 2026 rather than against aggregators, which are the usual source of stale deadlines and superseded award amounts. Where two sources disagreed the sponsor's page won: the Jane Coffin Childs stipend, for instance, is $50,000 a year flat, not the escalating scale that circulates elsewhere.

Deadlines are computed, not typed. Each fellowship stores its recurring pattern — a fixed calendar date, or a rule such as "fourth Tuesday in September" — and the page resolves the next occurrence when you load it. The dates below therefore stay correct into future cycles without anyone editing this file. What will drift is the money and the eligibility fine print, so treat the sponsor link as authoritative and this page as the thing that tells you a deadline is coming.

What is deliberately excluded. One-off calls, programs with no verifiable current cycle, awards requiring an independent faculty appointment (collected separately above), and internal institutional fellowships that are really recruitment vehicles for a single university.

Worth knowing separately: the NIH Extramural Loan Repayment Program is open to postdocs and repays up to $50,000 a year of educational debt. It is not a fellowship and does not compete with one — it can be held alongside. The cycle runs 1 September to 19 November each year. Citizenship or permanent residency is required.
